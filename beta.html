Beta

Cratis Studio is in beta, but your data is safe.

We are actively building and improving the product. Some features may change, and things may not always work exactly as expected. At the same time, we treat the service like production and operate it with full backup and recovery protections.

Important: Your data is handled as production data. We keep continuous backups, strong isolation, and restore-tested systems so you can try Cratis Studio without fear of losing content.

What beta means

Cratis Studio is available now for early users. During beta, we may add, change, or remove features quickly based on feedback. Some workflows may behave differently than they do in the final product.

What to focus on during beta

We want you to focus on what will give us the most valuable feedback:

  • Model your domain — Use Cratis Studio to express your event-driven systems naturally and completely, capturing all the detail you need for a full specification. Can you model everything you need to?
  • Verify the tools work — Test code generation, visualization, and modeling features. Make sure they're reliable and trustworthy so you can depend on them. Are all the tools working as expected?

Your feedback on these areas will help us build a stronger product. We're gearing toward the capability of running your models directly and generating production code that matches your intent.

Our next steps

While you focus on modeling and testing, we're working on:

  • Code generation — Expanding static code generation with AI-powered capabilities to help you generate code faster and with fewer errors.
  • UI support — Continuing to build a robust and intuitive interface that makes Cratis Studio a pleasure to use every day.

What you can expect

  • New features may arrive frequently.
  • Some interactions or UI details may move or be updated.
  • Beta features may be labeled preview or experimental.
  • We may change how certain capabilities work to make the product stronger.

What we guarantee

  • We treat your content as production data.
  • We run a full backup regime with point-in-time recovery.
  • Every organization's data is isolated and protected.
  • We verify restores so backups are usable when needed.

Pricing during beta

We are introducing transparent pricing in the billing section so you know exactly what to expect. Why this matters

Beta does not mean "fragile." It means we are improving fast while still respecting your trust. You can use Cratis Studio knowing we protect your data and keep your organization’s content recoverable.
