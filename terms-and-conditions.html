1. What the Service is

Cratis Studio is a hosted, multi-tenant application for modeling event-driven systems and collaborating on those models. It lets you and your colleagues describe domains, commands, events, read models and the flows between them, work on them together in real time, generate artifacts from them, and run them.

Each organization is a separate tenant. Your organization's models, events, and derived data are isolated from every other organization and are never pooled with, exposed to, or used on behalf of another customer.
